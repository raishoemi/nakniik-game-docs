### Overview
In my opinion the current state of favors (explained below) isn't sufficiently complex to accommodate strategies, progression, and so forth.
### Current State of Favors
Types of favors we thought of for now are:
- Give money, get favor
- Give favor, get favor
- Give multiple favors, get (a really good?) favor
### Idea
Future favors allow the player to "promise" something will happen.
For instance, fisherman comes along asking for wood for a new boat. To get wood you need a carpenter's favor which you **don't** have at the moment.
You can tell the fisherman you'll get him the wood sometime soon, and when you meet the carpenter later that day, or in a later day, you can get the wood to the fisherman and "complete" the favor - granting you the fisherman's favor.

I'm not yet sure how to utilize this for more complex strategies, but this removes the restriction that a player must have everything setup before meeting someone, so that they can have a useful interaction with them. It essentially permits more flexibility.
