[]()### "Royal Favor" Definition
A "Royal Favor" is a special type of favor that represents a "victory point" in the game. It signifies the player's growing influence and "pull" within the kingdom, earned by successfully completing high-stakes tasks or fulfilling the unique demands of Royal Members. Unlike regular favors, Royal Favors are rare and pivotal to achieving the ultimate goal of overthrowing the king.

### Transparency in Royal Favor Opportunities
As soon as a player first meets a Royal Member, the requirements for earning their Royal Favor will become known. This ensures that players can strategize effectively from the moment they encounter a Royal Member, allowing them to plan their actions and resource allocation accordingly.

### Examples of Royal Favor Opportunities
- **Tusik Smelly (Brothel Owner):**
  - Requirement: Flat sum of 30 coins.

- **Prince (Pompous Royal):**
  - Requirement: Enable a mischievous deed and blackmail him (requires a `GAY` and a `SPY`).

- **King's Chef (Culinary Enthusiast):**
  - Requirement: Provide rare ingredients for a sensational meal (e.g., a rare fish from a `SAILOR & MERCHANT` and a `SMUGGLER`).
