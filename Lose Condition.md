Your duty as an advisor is to serve the Royal Members.
Failing to answer their demands in any encounter will result in a strike.
3 strikes and you lose the game.

In every royal encounter you will have the following options
- Accept their original demands
- Decline and receive a strike
- *WIP Option* - RNG to avoid the encounter, if you succeed nothing happens, if you fail the result should be **worse** than just declining (risk/reward).
- Bargain - you will be presented with options depending on the current favors you have.
