### "Royal Favor" Definition
We need consistent wording on what a "victory point", a favor from a royal member is.
- ~~How to gain his confidence
- ~~How to gain his influence
- **Royal Favor (as opposed to regular/shit favor).**

### When are Royal Favors available to use
When meeting with a Royal Member you will always see a "Royal Favor Opportunity", which is greyed out until you have the required resources (coins, favors) to accomplish it.
**Coins as required resources** - Static amount
**Favors as required resources** - Can require one or multiple favors from certain groups (tags, e.g. "middle class", "chef", "merchant")

**In rare cases** - Royal Favors can be achieved with a single favor, that until you do it you wouldn't know it unlocks a Royal Favor - Essentially it isn't visible as greyed-out as a "Royal Favor Opportunity" so the player cannot plan for it (unless they already know about it).

**Obtaining multiple Royal Favors from the same Royal Member** - After receiving the 1st Royal Favor from a Royal Member, you may encounter them again and receive an additional favor, bigger and better and more difficult to obtain (but it also worth more).
